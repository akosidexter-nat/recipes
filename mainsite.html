Red Wine Sauce

This is a simple red wine sauce that I learned at the restaurant Grand Véranda.

Ingredients

  • Red wine
  • Carrots
  • Red onion
  • Garlic
  • Thyme
  • Tomato paste
  • Sugar
  • Cherry vinegar
  • Celeriac
  • Maizena
  • Beef stock
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt and Pepper

How to Make

  1. Cut the vegetables into small pieces and sauté them in a pot with oil
  2. Add sugar and caramelize it for a while
  3. Add the tomato paste and stir for a few minutes
  4. Pour in the wine, beef stock and a little water
  5. Reduce by half
  6. Season with salt and pepper
  7. Thicken with cornstarch to desired consistency

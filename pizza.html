Pizza Dough

Ingredients

  • Arencio — 3kg
  • Verde — 6kg
  • Olive oil — 300g
  • Salt — 250g
  • Dry yeast — 17.5g
  • Luke-warm water (28–32°C)

Instructions

  1. Put 90% of the water in the mixer
  2. In the remaining water, mix in the yeast
  3. Put all the flour in the mixer
  4. Mix the flour for 1 minute
  5. Add the yeast water and mix for 5 minutes
  6. Add the salt and mix for 5 minutes
  7. Add the olive oil and mix for 5 minutes
  8. Oil the table with olive oil so the dough doesn't stick
  9. Fold the dough
  10. Rest for 30 minutes
  11. Roll the dough into balls of 250g each
  12. Let it rise outside for 30–60 minutes
